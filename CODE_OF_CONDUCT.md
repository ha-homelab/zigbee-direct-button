# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and considerate regardless of background, identity or experience. Discuss technical evidence, welcome questions and protect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, trolling and publishing another person's private information are prohibited in project repositories, discussions and official communications.

Report concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com** with relevant links and context. The primary maintainer considers the parties' accounts, keeps reports confidential as far as practical, and may remove content, warn participants or restrict participation proportionately. Retaliation is prohibited. Affected participants may request reconsideration through the same private channel with additional evidence.

There is no independent conduct moderator. Complaints involving the maintainer cannot be promised independent review; GitHub's platform reporting facilities remain available for violations of its policies.
